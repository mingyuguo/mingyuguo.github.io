Profile

About Me

I am a Senior Lecturer, Program Director of the Master of Computer Science, and Graduate Research Coordinator in the School of Computer Science and Information Technology, Adelaide University.

I use algorithmic game theory and optimisation to study how AI systems make decisions when participants have competing interests. My research spans two themes:

Agentic Commerce and the Economics of Generative AI. I study how AI agents buy, sell and negotiate on behalf of people and organisations. Current work includes advertising in language-model responses, the procurement and pricing of AI services, and auction design for AI tokens.

AI for Drone and Cyber Defence. I develop algorithms for coordinating drone teams and protecting enterprise networks against cyber attacks. This work addresses how to choose effective strategies at scale while accounting for an adversary's responses, with support from defence and industry partners.

Before joining Adelaide, I was a Lecturer at the University of Liverpool. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Duke University, where my dissertation was runner-up for the Victor Lesser Distinguished Dissertation Award. I also hold an M.S. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Florida and a B.S. in Mathematics from Zhejiang University.

Research Partnerships

Industry Collaboration

I collaborate with industry and government on decision problems that combine AI, optimisation, and strategic interaction. Current and recent partnerships span defence and autonomous systems with Saab Australia and the Defence Science and Technology Group, cybersecurity with the Cyber Security Cooperative Research Centre, health operations with SA Pathology, and PropTech with DDDI Group.

I welcome conversations about collaborative research, industry PhDs, grant partnerships, and translating algorithms into decision-support tools and prototypes. Contact me to discuss a potential collaboration.
